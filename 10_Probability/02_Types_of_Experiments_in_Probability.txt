Types of Experiments in Probability
Types of Experiments
Deterministic – Always produces the same outcome when repeated under identical conditions.
Probabilistic / Random – Outcome is uncertain and may vary even if the experiment is repeated under identical conditions.
Sample Space
The sample space is the set of all possible outcomes of a probabilistic experiment.

Examples:

Rolling a Die → { 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 }
Tossing 2 Coins → { (HH), (HT), (TH), (TT) }
For Coins

Number of possible outcomes = 2ⁿ
(where n is the number of coins)
For Dice

Number of possible outcomes = 6ⁿ
(where n is the number of dice)
